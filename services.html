01
AI AUTOMATION

Spend less time on
repetitive work.

Many business processes involve the same manual tasks every day: reviewing information, sorting inquiries, processing documents, preparing drafts, and moving data between tools. MDE helps identify where AI and automation can make these processes faster and more consistent.

What we do: Design AI-assisted workflows, automate repetitive tasks, connect business tools, and introduce human review where accuracy and approval matter.

02
WEB DATA EXTRACTION

Turn scattered information
into usable data.

Valuable business information is often spread across websites, directories, catalogs, and other online sources. Finding it manually and organizing it into a consistent format can consume significant time. MDE helps collect information from appropriate sources and transform it into structured datasets.

What we do: Extract agreed data fields, standardize formats, identify duplicate records, validate results, and organize information for your intended use.

What you get: Structured data delivered in formats such as CSV, Excel, JSON, or a suitable database, depending on project requirements.

Business value: Spend less time copying information manually and make it easier to research markets, build lead lists, monitor publicly available information, and support business analysis.

03
DATA & BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE

Understand your numbers.
Make better decisions.

Data is most useful when it helps answer a business question. However, information stored across spreadsheets, reports, and different systems can be difficult to compare and interpret. MDE helps organize that information into a clearer view of business performance.

What we do: Clean and combine datasets, define relevant metrics, prepare recurring reports, and develop dashboards or visualizations based on your team's needs.

What you get: Organized datasets, clearly defined KPIs, repeatable reports, or dashboards that help communicate important trends and performance indicators.

Business value: Reduce time spent preparing reports, spot patterns more easily, and make decisions using more consistent information.

IT SYSTEMS & INTEGRATION

Make your tools work
together.

When business tools operate separately, teams often have to transfer information manually, repeat the same work, or depend on fragile processes. MDE helps create and connect digital systems so everyday operations become easier to manage.

What we do: Develop suitable internal tools, integrate compatible applications, connect data sources, and streamline workflows between systems.

What you get: A solution designed around your requirements, with agreed functionality, integration points, setup instructions, and handover documentation.

Business value: Reduce unnecessary tool switching, improve consistency between systems, and create workflows that are easier to maintain as your business grows.

05
SEO & GEO

Help the right people
find your business.

A business website needs more than an attractive design. Search engines and other discovery systems need to understand what the business offers, which pages answer specific questions, and how the website's information is organized. MDE helps improve these foundations through search-focused technical and content work.

What we do: Review technical SEO, improve page titles and descriptions, organize content and internal links, implement relevant structured data, and make business information clearer for search engines and AI-powered discovery systems.

What you get: Prioritized technical fixes, better organized landing pages, clearer service descriptions, relevant structured data, and content recommendations based on your goals.

Business value: Build a stronger foundation for organic discovery, help visitors understand your services, and make important business information easier to interpret and reference.

Important: Search rankings, AI citations, traffic, and leads depend on multiple factors. No specific placement or performance outcome can be guaranteed.

06
EMAIL AUTOMATION

Follow up consistently
without doing it all manually.

Important inquiries and customer follow-ups can be missed when communication depends entirely on manual reminders. MDE helps businesses organize recurring email tasks and create more consistent communication workflows.

What we do: Set up suitable email workflows, organize contact segments, connect forms and compatible business tools, and configure event-based messages and follow-up sequences.

What you get: Configured workflows, defined triggers, message templates, contact-handling rules, and a documented process for managing and reviewing email activity.

Business value: Reduce repetitive follow-up work, respond more consistently, and manage customer communication more systematically while respecting consent and unsubscribe preferences.
